Club History

Warriaga Park FC has been part of the local community for decades, built by players, families and volunteers.

Milestones

  1. 1985

    The club is founded by local families and plays its first matches on the park grounds.

  2. 1994

    The first junior team is formed, giving younger players in the area a place to play.

  3. 2003

    The club wins its first league title and the clubhouse is opened beside the main pitch.

  4. 2011

    A youth development programme is introduced, with volunteer coaches running weekly sessions.

  5. 2019

    Floodlights and improved changing facilities are added through fundraising and sponsorship.

  6. 2026

    The club website is rebuilt so that members, sponsors and supporters can follow the club online.

Our values

Community

Everyone in the area is welcome, whether they play, coach, volunteer or support from the sidelines.

Development

We give young players a pathway from junior football into the first team squad.

Volunteering

The club is run by volunteers, from match-day helpers to the committee.
